PANCHROMA CMYN / X-RITE i1Pro 2 RECORDING SHEET

Language: English | Simplified Chinese: measurements-i1pro2-guide.zh-CN.txt

Files and status
----------------
measurements-i1pro2.csv is a new, unmeasured reflection template.
The original measurements.csv is a general-purpose template for instrument or
camera measurements. Its Lab and spectral_file columns already support a
spectrophotometer; its RGB and capture_file columns support camera workflows.
Neither CSV contains or decodes RAW image data. This new file does not replace,
merge with, or automatically synchronize the original file.

Each sample has two backing modes and three planned repeats. White-backed
readings come first, followed by black-backed readings.
There is no backlit transmission section: a separate setup and protocol are
needed; reflection measurements must not be labeled as transmission.

CSV format: UTF-8, comma delimiter, dot decimal separator. Blank measurement
cells mean not measured, NOT zero. Preserve header names and identifiers.
Do not convert the reference percentages to spreadsheet percentage formatting:
25 means 25 percent, not 0.25. Scientific values may be negative for a_star/b_star.

Prefilled values
----------------
reading_id is unique, for example TC01-W-01 (white backing, first planned read)
or TC01-B-01 (black backing, first planned read).
sample_id, plate, role, design_thickness_mm and C/M/Y/N_percent are copied from
samples.csv. They describe the generated design, NOT observed extrusion ratios
or measured thickness. N is Grey, not Black. Keep samples.csv available for the
full layout and explicit layer sequence.

face=top, rotation_degrees=0, illuminant=D50, observer=1931_2 and
instrument=X-Rite i1Pro 2 are PROTOCOL DEFAULTS, not evidence of actual setup.
Check and change them to match each reading. In this protocol, top means the
printed +Z face; bottom means the build-plate face. Zero rotation is the chosen
reference orientation with the clipped corner used to reproduce placement.
The repeat column reserves three reads; it does not assert that any were taken.

The generator leaves measurement_condition blank. A run may prefill a chosen
acquisition condition, such as native M2, for unmeasured rows. Such a prefill is a
protocol default, not evidence that the instrument used that condition.
Record the actual acquisition condition and any simulation/FWA compensation;
never relabel existing readings to match a new protocol.
D50/1931_2 describes colorimetric computation, not native M1 or M2 acquisition.
ArgyllCMS documents that its i1Pro 2 driver does not use the device UV mode;
do not label a software simulation as a native M1 or M2 measurement or pool it
with a native dataset without a separate comparability study.
M0/M1/M2 datasets are different acquisition conditions, not independent repeats.

Fill once per setup, then copy to all applicable rows
---------------------------------------------------
print_run: identify the actual print job, for example a date plus plate ID.
measurement_session: a unique name for one instrument calibration/setup session.
measurement_condition: actual M condition and any compensation, when known.
backing_id: identify the actual white or black backing; do not combine materials.
software: application and version used to acquire/export the data.
instrument_serial: the serial number of the instrument actually used.
illuminant / observer: confirm that these match the exported Lab computation.
Use notes for calibration time, changed settings, uncertainty and anomalies.
Retain the matching G-code/process settings and spool batch records with each
print_run. Start a new measurement_session after changing acquisition conditions.

Fill for each reading
---------------------
L_star, a_star, b_star: the measured Lab values, as three numeric cells.
Keep the instrument/software precision; do not invent additional digits.
If a reading fails or was not taken, leave all three blank and explain in notes.

spectral_file: path to the exported spectrum for this particular reading,
relative to this directory or absolute. Leave blank until the file actually
exists. A useful filename convention is spectra/TC01-W-01.sp, provided that the
software exports that format. Other native spectral formats are also acceptable.
Do not rename an incompatible file merely to give it a .sp extension.
Exporting Lab alone does not preserve the spectrum. Preserve wavelength labels,
units and original spectral files; the CSV stores references, not spectral bins.

actual_thickness_mm: independently measured sample thickness, not the design
value. Measure without crushing thin samples. If one thickness determination is
reused for repeated optical reads, copying it is acceptable; note that it was
one thickness measurement rather than three independent measurements.

face / rotation_degrees: verify the actual viewed face and orientation.
measured_at: record the acquisition time if available, preferably ISO 8601 with
time zone, or preserve it in the associated instrument export/session record.
notes: bubbles, contamination, warping, repositioning problems, rejected reads,
or anything needed to interpret the data. Do not silently remove outliers.

Measurement procedure
---------------------
1. Calibrate the instrument with its original calibration base as instructed by
   the software. A sample backing is not the instrument calibration white tile.
2. Set the intended software measurement mode. Confirm the Lab illuminant and
   observer. Keep the sample/backing contact and instrument geometry consistent.
3. Start with white-backed reflection. Use spot measurements in the central
   area of each 20 x 20 mm coupon, away from clipped corners, walls and seams.
4. Take repeat 1, lift/reposition the instrument, and take repeats 2 and 3 at the
   same nominal orientation and central region. Do not enter one measurement
   into all three repeat rows.
5. Measure black-backed reflection as a separate group when required. It is not
   necessary to complete both modes before analyzing the white-backed pilot.
6. For a rotation or opposite-face experiment, append new rows with unique
   reading_id values and the actual orientation/face. Do not overwrite the
   original repetitions. For a reprint, use a different print_run and reading_id.
7. Save the raw readings before averaging. Compute separate summaries for each
   sample, backing, face, orientation and comparable measurement condition.

Backing definitions and bare-backing readings
--------------------------------------------
backings.csv identifies the physical backings. The generated IDs WB01 (white)
and BB01 (black) are protocol defaults, not certified reference materials.
Fill material, brand/source, batch, sheet count, thickness, surface finish,
used face and underlay as applicable. Leave unknown properties blank.
Changing the material, paper stack, used face or underlay requires a new
backing_id; do not redefine an ID already associated with real readings.

measurements-i1pro2.csv links each coupon reading to backings.csv by backing_id.
Confirm the prefilled ID against the backing actually used before recording.
Neither backing_id nor measurement_session is an automatically verified value.

backing-references-i1pro2.csv contains only measurements of bare backings,
without a coupon. It reserves 12 reads for one session: each of two backings
at session_phase=start and end, with three repositioned repeats each.
Lab remains in columns E/F/G. Lab and spectral-file cells remain blank until
measured/exported. Any prefilled acquisition condition is only a run default;
verify the actual instrument and colorimetric settings against the coupon rows.
These are not calibration-tile or dark-current reads.

Fill measurement_session with the same session ID used for the corresponding
coupon readings. Join using BOTH backing_id and measurement_session, and compare
only matching measurement_condition, illuminant, observer and instrument setup.
The prefilled position=reference and rotation_degrees=0 are placement defaults:
identify a repeatable physical region and record the actual placement.
Keep the backing stack, underlay and instrument-to-surface distance consistent.

Take the start controls after instrument calibration and before the coupons;
take the end controls after the coupon readings. Repeat rows require independent
repositioned measurements, not copied values. Preserve outliers with explanatory
notes. Export individual spectra or, for a multi-reading export, record each
row's original export SampleID in notes as well as the shared spectral_file.

For another session, append rows with a new measurement_session and unique
reading_id values, for example S02-WB01-START-01. Do not overwrite the first
session's controls or reuse its identifiers. Additional positions can be
recorded using session_phase=uniformity_check and distinct position labels and
reading IDs; do not average different positions into the reference controls.

Keep backing controls out of samples.csv, recipe fitting and holdout evaluation.
Do not assign filament percentages or treat them as zero-thickness coupons.
Use their spectra to assess backing color, uniformity and session stability.
Do not subtract backing Lab from coupon Lab or assume direct spectral subtraction
isolates the filament: reflection includes filtering, scattering and multiple
reflections. A defined optical model is needed for such an inversion.

An optically neutral certified backing is not a prerequisite for an empirical
run-specific calibration. If copier paper is the available white backing, keep
the same identified stack and record its bare spectrum. Native M2 can reduce UV-
excited fluorescence but does not guarantee a neutral or fluorescence-free white.
Keep coupon readings and both bare backings in the same acquisition condition.
Do not set paper to Lab(100, 0, 0), clip values above 100 percent, or subtract its
blue cast from coupon Lab. Keep raw exports; a different illumination or physical
backing needs its own validation. Do not silently change an established run.

Analysis boundaries
-------------------
Do not pool white- and black-backed readings, or reflective and backlit data.
Use mix-fit for fitting and reserve mix-holdout for independent validation;
keep mix-holdout out of model fitting and tuning.
Repeated reads of one coupon assess capture repeatability; duplicate printed
coupons and independent reprints assess different sources of variation.
A measured reflection spectrum includes the sample, backing and measurement
geometry. It is not an intrinsic absorption or total-transmission spectrum.
